# Guzara Allowance Policy

> **MOCK SPECIMEN - NOT AN OFFICIAL DOCUMENT.** The Zakat and Welfare Board named
> here is a simulated body created for the Haqdaar prototype. No real board,
> fund, or programme is described.

## 1. Purpose of the Allowance

The Guzara Allowance is a monthly subsistence allowance for the poorest
households in the province, regardless of the applicant's marital status. It is
means-tested: the household must be below both a poverty score threshold and an
income threshold. The allowance is meant to cover food and basic household needs,
and it is paid to the household's primary caregiver.

## 2. Who Is Eligible

An applicant is eligible when **all** of the following are true:

1. The applicant is alive in the civil identity registry and the record is active.
2. The CNIC is valid and has not expired.
3. The applicant is at least 18 years old.
4. The household **poverty score is 30 or below** on the proxy means test.
5. Monthly household income is **PKR 30,000 or less**.
6. The household does not already receive a conflicting relief grant.
7. The applicant lives in one of the ten eligible Sindh districts.
8. The CNIC photograph has been verified.

The poverty score comes from the household survey conducted by a visiting
enumerator. If no survey exists for the household, the poverty score is treated
as **unknown**, not as zero. An unknown score sends the case to a human officer
rather than approving it on an assumption.

## 3. How the Amount Is Calculated

The allowance is built from a base amount plus a per-child supplement:

- Base amount: **PKR 8,000 per month**
- Per child under 18: **PKR 500 per month**
- Maximum payable: **PKR 12,000 per month**

So a household with no children receives PKR 8,000, a household with four
children receives PKR 10,000, and a household with eight or more children
receives the capped PKR 12,000. The amount is recalculated whenever the number of
children under 18 changes, and the citizen is told the new amount in the app.

## 4. Required Documents

Only the applicant's **CNIC front side** is mandatory. A household survey record
is fetched from the registry by the system; the citizen does not need to bring a
paper copy. If the survey is missing, the officer may request a fresh visit
instead of asking the citizen for more paperwork.

صرف درخواست گزار کے شناختی کارڈ کا سامنے والا حصہ لازمی ہے۔ گھریلو سروے کا ریکارڈ
نظام خود حاصل کرتا ہے، شہری کو کاغذی نقل لانے کی ضرورت نہیں۔ سروے موجود نہ ہونے پر

## 5. Income Conflicts

The income a citizen declares in the app is compared with the income recorded in
the household survey. If the two differ by more than the tolerance, the case is
flagged as an income conflict and reviewed by an officer. A conflict is **not**
treated as fraud: survey data is often years old, and a household's circumstances
change. The officer sees both numbers and decides which is more credible.

## 6. Decision Rules and Human Review

The allowance uses the same published rule engine as every other programme. Each
rule is evaluated to pass, fail, or unknown. Automatic approval requires every
required rule to pass and overall confidence of at least 0.90. Unknown facts are
never replaced with defaults, and the system has no automatic rejection path.

A human officer is the only actor who may reject. The officer's decision, the
reason, and the evidence are all recorded in the audit trail, and the citizen can
read the reason in plain Urdu or English.

یہ وظیفہ بھی وہی شائع شدہ قواعد استعمال کرتا ہے۔ ہر قاعدہ پورا، ناکام یا معلوم نہیں

## 7. Complaints and Grievances

Any citizen may file a complaint about the allowance without paying anything. The
complaint is given a reference number, and the citizen can track it in the app.
Complaints about a helper, about a delay longer than thirty days, or about a
payment that never arrived are escalated to a supervisor automatically.

اور شہری اسے ایپ میں دیکھ سکتا ہے۔ مددگار کے رویے، تیس دن سے زیادہ تاخیر، یا نہ پہنچنے
