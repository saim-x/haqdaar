# Sahara Widow Grant Policy

> **MOCK SPECIMEN - NOT AN OFFICIAL DOCUMENT.** This policy text is synthetic and
> exists only to demonstrate the Haqdaar prototype. It is not legal advice and it
> does not describe any real government programme.

## 1. Purpose of the Grant

The Sahara Widow Grant is a monthly cash transfer for widowed women living in the
ten eligible districts of Sindh. It exists so that a household that has lost its
primary earner does not fall into destitution while the family adjusts. The grant
is paid to the widow herself, into an account or mobile wallet registered in her
own name.

سہارا بیوہ گرانٹ سندھ کے دس مستحق اضلاع میں رہنے والی بیوہ خواتین کے لیے ماہانہ مالی
مدد ہے۔ یہ گرانٹ خاندان کے سربراہ کے انتقال کے بعد گھر کو مفلسی سے بچانے کے لیے دی
جاتی ہے۔ رقم بیوہ خاتون کے اپنے نام کے اکاؤنٹ یا موبائل والٹ میں بھیجی جاتی ہے۔

## 2. Who Is Eligible

An applicant is eligible when **all** of the following are true:

1. The applicant is alive in the civil identity registry and her record is active.
2. Her CNIC is valid and has not expired.
3. She is at least 18 years old.
4. The death of her spouse is confirmed either by the civil death register or by
   a verified death certificate.
5. Monthly household income is **PKR 40,000 or less**.
6. She does not already receive a conflicting relief grant from another programme.
7. She lives in one of these districts: Thatta, Badin, Hyderabad, Karachi,
   Tharparkar, Umerkot, Mirpurkhas, Sukkur, Larkana, or Dadu.
8. Her CNIC photograph has been verified by the document checker.

If any of these facts is **unknown** rather than false, the application is not
rejected. It is either sent back to the applicant for one more answer, or passed
to a human officer. Haqdaar never rejects an application automatically.

## 3. Required Documents

Two documents are required:

- **CNIC front side** of the applicant. The card must be photographed flat, in
  good light, with all four corners visible. A photograph forwarded through
  WhatsApp is acceptable as long as the security code can still be read.
- **Spouse death certificate**, or a civil death record reference. If the death
  register already confirms the death, the certificate is not mandatory.

Screenshots of a screenshot, heavily cropped images, and photographs taken in the
dark will be sent back with a spoken instruction to retake the picture. A retake
request is **not** an accusation of forgery.

دو دستاویزات ضروری ہیں: درخواست گزار کے شناختی کارڈ کا سامنے والا حصہ، اور شوہر کا
وفات سرٹیفکیٹ یا سول ریکارڈ کا حوالہ۔ تصویر اچھی روشنی میں، کارڈ کے چاروں کونے
نمایاں ہوں، ایسی ہونی چاہیے۔ واٹس ایپ سے بھیجی گئی تصویر بھی قابلِ قبول ہے بشرطیکہ

## 4. Payout Schedule and Amount

The grant is paid monthly. The base amount is **PKR 12,000 per month**. The
payment is initiated automatically once the application is approved, and the
citizen receives an SMS with a verification code when the money leaves the
department. Payment normally reaches a mobile wallet within one working day.

If a payment fails - for example because the wallet account is dormant or the
account title does not match the applicant's name - the case is flagged for an
officer. The citizen is never asked to pay a fee to release the grant.

گرانٹ ماہانہ دی جاتی ہے۔ بنیادی رقم بارہ ہزار روپے ماہانہ ہے۔ منظوری کے بعد ادائیگی
خودکار طور پر شروع ہو جاتی ہے اور رقم بھیجتے وقت شہری کو تصدیقی کوڈ کے ساتھ پیغام

## 5. How the Decision Is Made

Every application is checked against the same published rule set. Each rule
records whether it passed, failed, or could not be determined. The system may
approve an application automatically only when every required rule has passed
**and** overall confidence is at least 0.90. Anything less goes to a human
officer, who sees the full rule trace and the evidence behind each fact.

A human officer is the only actor who can reject an application. The system
itself has no reject action at all.

ہر درخواست ایک ہی شائع شدہ قواعد کے مطابق جانچی جاتی ہے۔ ہر قاعدہ بتاتا ہے کہ وہ پورا
ہوا، ناکام ہوا، یا معلوم نہیں رہا۔ خودکار منظوری صرف اس صورت میں ممکن ہے جب تمام

## 6. Complaint and Appeal Rights

A citizen may complain about a delay, a wrong decision, or the behaviour of a
helper at any time, free of charge. Complaints are logged with a reference number
and must be acknowledged. A citizen may also ask for the reasons behind a
decision in plain language, and may ask for a human review of any automated step.

If a citizen believes her application was wrongly refused, she may appeal once
within thirty days. The appeal is reviewed by a different officer than the one
who made the original decision.

کا حوالہ نمبر دیا جاتا ہے اور اس کا اعتراف ضروری ہے۔ شہری فیصلے کی وجوہات آسان زبان

## 7. If You Do Not Have a CNIC

If you do not have a CNIC, do not enter another person's number or make one up.
This prototype cannot complete a Sahara application without the applicant's own
valid CNIC and a readable photograph of its front side. Ask a community helper or
department officer for assistance, and contact NADRA for current instructions on
first-time registration. Haqdaar cannot issue a CNIC or promise that an
application can proceed without one. Missing identity information must be
handled by a person; the system must not automatically reject the applicant.

## 8. What This Grant Is Not

The Sahara Widow Grant is not a loan and does not have to be repaid. It is not
inherited and it does not pass to the children automatically. It does not require
membership of any political party, and no agent is entitled to a commission from
it. Anyone who asks for money to "process" this grant is committing fraud.

خودبخود بچوں کو نہیں ملتی۔ اس کے لیے کسی سیاسی جماعت کی رکنیت ضروری نہیں اور کسی
