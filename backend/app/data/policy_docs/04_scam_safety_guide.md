# Scam Safety Guide

> **MOCK SPECIMEN — NOT AN OFFICIAL DOCUMENT.** This guide is synthetic and exists
> only to demonstrate the Haqdaar prototype. It is not legal advice.

## 1. The One Rule That Catches Most Frauds

**No genuine benefit ever requires you to pay money to receive money.** If a
message asks for a processing fee, a file charge, a tax deposit, or a "release
payment" before your grant arrives, it is fraud. There is no exception to this
rule, and no officer will ever ask you to break it.

پیغام گرانٹ ملنے سے پہلے پروسیسنگ فیس، فائل چارج، ٹیکس یا "رقم جاری کرنے" کے پیسے
مانگے تو وہ فراڈ ہے۔ اس اصول کا کوئی استثنا نہیں اور کوئی افسر آپ سے ایسا نہیں کہے گا۔

## 2. How Official Verification Codes Work

Every genuine message from the department carries a short verification code, for
example `HQ-7K2M4`. The code is generated from the exact text of the message, so
it changes whenever the message changes. To check a message:

1. Open the Haqdaar app and go to the scam checker.
2. Paste the message, or photograph the SMS, or record a voice note.
3. The checker compares the code with the codes the department actually issued.
4. If the code matches an issued message **and** the message text matches, the
   checker says the message is official.

A code that does not exist, or a code that belongs to a different message, is a
red flag. Fraudsters copy a real code from one message and paste it into another;
the mismatch is exactly what the checker detects.

## 3. Common Fraud Tactics

Fraudsters reuse the same handful of tricks. Learn to recognise them:

- **The fee trap.** "Send PKR 1,500 to release your PKR 25,000 grant." Real grants
  never require an advance payment.
- **The OTP theft.** "Tell me the code we just sent you so I can verify you." The
  one-time password is for you alone. Anyone who asks for it is stealing access.
- **The PIN request.** "Confirm your wallet PIN to receive the money." No
  department ever needs your PIN. Money arrives without it.
- **The urgency threat.** "Your grant expires in two hours." Deadlines are used to
  stop you from checking. Real programmes do not expire in hours.
- **The fake officer.** A caller claims to be from the department and reads out
  your CNIC to sound convincing. Ask for a reference number and call the official
  helpline yourself.
- **The personal account.** "Send the money to this mobile number." Department
  payments come from the department, never from a private wallet.
- **The lookalike link.** A link that almost matches the official address, with an
  extra letter or a different ending. Do not open it; type the address yourself.

## 4. What the Checker Can and Cannot Do

The scam checker is **advisory**. It gives you a verdict — likely scam, cannot
tell, likely safe, or verified official — with the reasons behind it. It never
blocks your account, never deletes a message, and never changes the status of your
application. If it says "cannot tell", that is an honest answer: short messages
and forwarded screenshots often do not contain enough evidence either way.

When the checker is unsure, do not act on the message. Ask a community helper, or
call the department's helpline, or visit an office in person. Being slow is safe;
being fast is what fraudsters want.

## 5. If You Have Already Been Defrauded

Act quickly and do not blame yourself. Fraud is designed to work on careful people.

1. Do not send any more money, and do not share any further codes.
2. Call your mobile wallet provider and ask them to freeze the transaction.
3. Report the number to the department's helpline and keep the message.
4. File a complaint in the app; it will be given a reference number.
5. Tell a helper or a family member so that the same trick is not repeated.

Your benefit application is **not** cancelled because you were defrauded. Being a
victim of fraud never makes you ineligible for a grant.

## 6. Protecting Your Documents

Your CNIC photograph is as sensitive as your PIN. Do not send it to strangers on
WhatsApp, do not post it in groups, and do not let a helper keep it on their own
phone. When you upload it to Haqdaar, the app reads the security code on the card
to confirm the card is genuine, and it stores only what the application needs.

## 7. Where to Get Help

Help is free. A community helper can sit with you and go through the app step by
step. The department helpline can confirm whether a message is genuine. An office
visit is always allowed, and no one may refuse to serve you because you came in
person instead of using the app.
